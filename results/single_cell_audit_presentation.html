What I built

An interrogable audit, not a static conclusion

Light-mode dashboard with an active Color map by workflow.

Uppercase query normalization prevents case bugs: cd3d → CD3D.

UMAP, continuous expression overlays, cluster markers, and quality fields remain queryable.

The result

Cluster 4 is a highly viable, standard population of blood platelets and megakaryocyte fragments, not a novel cell line.

What I checked · the controls

Quality and lineage agree

2.4%low mito: no stress signal
1,263transcripts: not empty droplets
PPBPplatelet marker
PF4platelet marker

Significant PPBP and PF4 expression maps Cluster 4 to platelet lineage.

What I would not claim

Scope is not replication

This is a single-donor repository analysis.

Neighbor weights and resolution were not systematically varied; either could fold these cells into monocyte profiles.

No multi-donor novelty claim.

Next steps

Test the call outside this matrix

1. Validate PPBP/PF4 and the full signature against independent public references, including the Human Cell Atlas.

2. Ask whether density-gradient platelet depletion was omitted during sample preparation.

Until then: platelet fragments, investigate - not novel immune population.
